# POLAR @ SemEval-2026

**Task 9: Detecting Multilingual, Multicultural, and Multievent Online Polarization**

## Project Overview

Online polarization refers to the sharp divisions and hostility between social, political, or identity groups in online spaces. It is a growing concern, as it often precedes hate speech, offensive discourse, and social fragmentation. In extreme cases, polarization can lead to a fragmented society where individuals or groups are unable to engage in constructive dialogue, undermining community cohesion and social unity.

This project participates in **Task 9 at SemEval-2026**, which introduces a **polarization detection challenge**. The task focuses on identifying multilingual, multicultural, and multievent polarization, reflecting the complexity of online discourse across diverse contexts.

Participants may work on one or more of the following sub-tasks:

1. **Polarization Detection** – Determine whether a post or comment exhibits polarization.
2. **Polarization Type Classification** – Identify the type of polarization present.
3. **Polarization Manifestation Identification** – Recognize how polarization is manifested in the content.

The goal of this project is to build models and pipelines capable of accurately detecting and classifying online polarization in multiple languages and cultural contexts.
